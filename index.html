AI transparency

Every demo here is a scripted, self-contained browser simulation. Nothing you type is sent to an AI model and no live AI system runs behind any of them, even where a demo plays one. Their code and copy were built with generative AI (Anthropic’s Claude) and reviewed by Robert Barcik, who is responsible for what is published (LearningDoe s.r.o.). This is a voluntary disclosure in the spirit of Article 50 of the EU AI Act; each demo repeats it in the small “AI transparency” tab in its corner.
