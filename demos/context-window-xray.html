You type a sentence. What does the model actually receive?

Most people assume the text they type is all the LLM sees. It isn't — not even close. Modern AI apps engineer the context window: on every single call they assemble a fresh payload of system instructions, retrieved memories, skill catalogs, tool definitions, uploaded documents, search results, and conversation history — and your message is usually the smallest part of it. Takeaway
The model is stateless. The app is the author.
  • Every call starts from zero — "memory" means the app re-packs everything it wants the model to know, every single time.
  • Across six turns Maya typed a few hundred tokens; the app shipped tens of thousands. Most of the prompt is text she never wrote and never sees.
  • Context is a budget: memories are retrieved selectively (and flagged as possibly stale), skills load lazily, documents get truncated, and old turns are silently compacted away. Deciding what enters the window, and at what fidelity, is context engineering — it is most of what makes a GenAI app good.
🔁 Try it again on an 8K window (top right) — watch how much the app has to sacrifice, and how early. Then compare with 200K: nothing is lost, but every call re-sends ~15,000 tokens.
